# 06 UX

## Flows

### Officer, first release
1. Open app, pick "I am the sub-county officer", enter demo passcode.
2. Board loads with the seed season in `open`, subsidy off. Strip is red: "Farmers will be told not to travel". Every row red, blocking "Subsidy not yet released".
3. Officer flips "Subsidy released". Board recomputes instantly (client calls /api/board). Rows turn to their real colours.
4. Officer taps a yellow row, sees "Validated by the local Assistant Chief: pending". Toggles signed, enters "Chief Kamau". Row goes green.
5. Presses "Release to 6 farmers". Confirm sheet. "Send now". Progress. Result: 4 sent, 1 demo skipped, 1 no phone.
6. Board shows delivery icons. Done.

### Farmer, receiving
1. WhatsApp message arrives: colour word first, depot, amount, pending items, link.
2. Taps link, status page opens in Swahili. Sees green, "Go to NCPB Kitale. Carry your ID and Ksh 2,500 per bag."
3. At the depot, taps "Show at the counter", hands the phone over or holds it up.

### Proxy, relaying
1. Same message, taps "Read this to the farmer", reads the three lines aloud.
2. Forwards the message to the farmer's basic phone with Share (SMS text, no link needed).

### Officer, approval lands after release
1. Chief signs for Chebet. Officer toggles signed. Row marked "changed".
2. "Send updates (1)" appears. Press. Only Chebet gets a new message.

### Anyone, no smartphone
1. Dial the USSD code. "0. My status". Enter ID digits. Read the verdict.

## Interaction rules
- One primary action per screen. On the board it is Release or Send updates. On the farmer page it is Show at the counter.
- Every destructive or outward action (release, send updates, subsidy off) confirms with counts, never a bare "Are you sure".
- Toggles save immediately and show a spinner on the toggle itself, never a page-level loader. Revert on failure with a toast.
- Filters and sorts are instant, client side, no animation.
- The farmer page never asks the farmer to do anything except go or not go. All editing is officer side.
- Language: farmer page opens in the language the message was sent in; toggle remains.
- Back button always works because every screen is a hash route. Sheets close on back.

## Micro-interactions
- Row colour bar changes colour with a 160ms colour transition when an approval flips; no movement.
- Delivery icon changes state without animation; a sent icon is a fact, not an event.
- Release progress bar is linear and honest; it advances per farmer completed, not on a timer.
- Toast for saved approval: "Marked signed by Chief Kamau", 1.8s.

## Copy rules
- Verdict words stay: Utapata / Served, Bado kuna kitu / Conditional, Hutapata / Not served. Directly under the word, one action line in plain language: green "Go to <depot>. Carry your ID and Ksh <price> per bag.", yellow "Do not travel yet. First: <pending items>.", red "Do not travel. <reason>." Decided 2 Sep, Q1.
- Every number on the farmer page has a source or "set by sub-county office".
- No em dashes anywhere. Tested.
