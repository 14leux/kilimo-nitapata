# 05 UI

Same visual system as the v2 refresh (Manrope, JetBrains Mono for IDs, green primary, verdict colours ok/warn/danger, 16 to 20px radii, 44px minimum touch targets after the Codex fixes). Mobile first at 375, officer board also comfortable at 768 and up.

## Hierarchy rule
On any screen the first thing the eye lands on is the verdict colour and word. Second is the single action. Third is the reason. Everything else is below the fold.

## S1 Mode picker (first open)
Two full-width cards, 120px tall each, icon left, title 20px, one line of copy 15px. Officer card second and visually quieter (surface-2 background). Below: "Remembered on this phone. Change any time from the top bar." 13px.

## S2 Officer board
- Top bar: brand, season name as a pill, mode switch.
- Season strip (card, 72px): season status chip, "Subsidy released" toggle with label, price per bag with edit pencil. If subsidy is off, strip background is danger-bg and the text says "Farmers will be told not to travel".
- Filter chips row (44px tall chips, counts in mono): All 6, Green 2, Yellow 3, Red 1, Not reached.
- Rows (72px): 8px colour bar on the left edge (ok/warn/danger), name 17px bold, ID and depot 13px, blocking item 14px on the right in ink-2, delivery icon far right (paper plane grey = not sent, green = sent, red = failed, dashed = demo).
- Sticky bottom: primary button "Release to 6 farmers" or "Send updates (2)". Disabled state carries the reason as the label.
- Empty and loading states: skeleton rows, three of them, no spinner.

## S3 Farmer detail sheet (officer)
Bottom sheet, 88vh. Header: name, ID, colour pill. Sections in cards: Requirements (ledger rows as today), Approvals (each row: title, signed pill or "pending" pill, signer and date 13px, toggle switch 44px on the right), Allocation, Cost lines, Messages (list, newest first, channel icon, status, time). Footer button: "Send status to this farmer".

## S4 Release confirm sheet
Title 20px: "Send status to 6 farmers". Three stat tiles: Green 2, Yellow 3, Red 1, each with its colour. Line 15px: "By WhatsApp and SMS. Demo phones are skipped. This cannot be unsent." Buttons: primary "Send now", ghost "Not yet".

## S5 Release progress
Same sheet morphs: progress bar (linear, 4px, primary), "Sending 3 of 6", then a result list with per-farmer status and a "Retry failed (1)" secondary button if needed.

## S6 Farmer status page
Existing answer screen order, with additions:
1. Verdict card (as today) plus a line "Released by sub-county office, 2 Sep 14:05" 13px mono.
2. Action line card: one sentence, 17px bold, e.g. "Go to NCPB Kitale. Carry your ID and Ksh 2,500 per bag."
3. What is missing (as today), now listing every pending item when two or fewer.
4. Approvals card: rows with signed or pending pill, signer name.
5. Cost card: rows, price per bag, bags remaining, total to pay (20px bold), subsidy note, "No other fee" line, each with citation.
6. Action buttons as today: Show at the counter, Share, Read this to the farmer, Check another.
7. USSD line, sample data line.

## S7 Counter view (farmer)
As today plus, under the price: "Total for 1 bag: Ksh 2,500" 24px, and the release timestamp line. The verdict word sizes as today.

## S8 Offline and freshness badges
Top bar pill, 28px: "Offline, as of 14:05" (warn-bg) or "Newer status available" (primary, tappable).

## Dimensions and density
- Base grid 8px. Cards 16 to 18px padding. Section gaps 16px.
- Row heights: board 72px, ledger 56px, sheet options 56px.
- Type: 11px mono eyebrows, 13px meta, 15px body secondary, 17px body, 20px titles, verdict word fitted.

## Motion (Emil rules)
- Sheets 260ms drawer curve, backdrop 220ms. Press scale 0.97 at 160ms. Board row colour bar does not animate. Progress bar is linear. Reduced motion: none, not different.
