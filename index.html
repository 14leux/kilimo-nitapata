About this demo

A readiness explainer, not a live government service.

The prototype shows how a farmer or family member could understand record requirements, depot verification and conflicting price evidence before making a trip.

What it checks

Sample farmer requirements and a static list of demonstration depots.

What it cannot know

Live stock, opening hours, current voucher validity or final depot approval.

What it never does

No buying, selling, agronomic advice or movement of money.
