How this tool decides & where its data comes from

The six requirements in sections 01 to 03 and the two prices in section 04 are transcribed from the five published news and analysis sources listed in section 05, not from the underlying gazette notice or the KIAMIS system's own user manual, which were not publicly retrievable at build time. Where sources disagree (as with price), both figures are shown with their date and source rather than one being picked as correct.

The farmer and depot records in the two dropdowns above are sample data built for this demo, not a live feed from KIAMIS or any county register. Swapping in a real registry lookup and an authoritative document set would be the next step past a hackathon build.

The match between a farmer and a depot is decided by a fixed rule table, not a language model: each requirement is checked against the selected farmer's record, each depot carries its own authorisation flag, and the verdict is whichever is worse of the two. Nothing is inferred or guessed.
