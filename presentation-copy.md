# Why this needs to exist: persona copy

For the presentation. One slide's worth of copy per persona, plus the line that ties them together. Pulled from the team's own problem and user document, tightened for delivery, not restated.

---

## The line that ties it together

The person typing the question, the person deciding whether to travel, and the person who has to be convinced at the counter are three different people. Most tools like this get built for the first one and quietly fail the other two.

---

## The farmer

*Nikienda leo, nitapata?* If I go today, will I get it?

He's already enrolled. The scheme already has his name, his acreage, his crop. None of that is in question. What's in question is whether he can see any of it before he spends a day and a fare finding out the hard way. He doesn't think in terms of eligibility. He thinks in terms of "they said we'd get fertiliser." When it doesn't work out, he doesn't just lose the day. He loses the ability to tell, once he's standing at the counter, whether the price he's being asked for is the real one.

## The proxy

He's the one actually holding the phone.

Most agricultural tools get designed for a farmer typing his own question into an app. In practice, it's often his son, his daughter, a grandchild, the person who already runs the household's WhatsApp and M-Pesa. He asks on the farmer's behalf, reads the answer, and has to relay it accurately enough, by voice, in another language, that an older relative is willing to make the trip on the strength of it. Design past him, and the tool fails quietly, not because the logic was wrong, but because nobody built for the person actually running it.

## The depot clerk

Not a user. The person the answer has to survive.

He's not opening this app. He's standing at his own counter, with his own register, deciding whether to serve the farmer in front of him and what to actually charge. The citation in this product isn't there to look thorough. It's there for this one moment: a farmer holding up a screen with a specific number, from a specific dated source, either corrects a mistake or starts a confrontation, and the farmer is the one who absorbs the consequences either way. The design has to earn the first outcome.

## The extension officer

She's already answering this question. All day, one farmer at a time.

She's the county desk, the barraza speaker, the person who fields the same handful of questions dozens of times a week. If this tool makes her faster, adoption doesn't happen one download at a time. It happens in groups, the moment she starts using it in front of the people she already talks to. She's also the only one of the four who can tell us which published document is actually the operative one this season, which isn't always the most recently issued one. She's not just distribution. She's ground truth about the sources themselves.
